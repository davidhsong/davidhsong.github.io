Fishing

I fish the Bay Area coast, from piers and shore mostly, and offshore when I can get on a boat. Everything below is within driving distance.

Spots

  • Bodega Bay North of San Francisco. Fishes well from both land and boat. Surfperch, salmon, rockfish.
  • Half Moon Bay The main jumping-off point for offshore trips. Halibut, lingcod, salmon, sharks.
  • Pacifica Pier and Johnson Pier Easy pier fishing with basic gear. Striped bass, flounder, salmon, sturgeon.
  • Fort Baker and Crissy Field Shore spots in Marin and San Francisco. Striped bass, halibut, leopard sharks.
  • San Pablo Bay and Sausalito Good surf and shore water. Striped bass, salmon, halibut.
  • Berkeley Flats and Emeryville Piers and shore. Striped bass and sturgeon.
  • Lake Merced Local spot in San Francisco. Bass and a few other species.
